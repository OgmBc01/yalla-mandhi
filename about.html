The Beginning

Our Humble Beginnings

A Family Legacy

Founded with a passion for authentic Yemani cuisine, Yalla Al Mandhi brings generations of culinary tradition to your table. Our journey began with the desire of Mr. Zayed to share the traditional aroma of Yemani dishes with friends and the world..

In 2015, brothers Abbas and Mohammad brought these cherished family recipes to Dubai, determined to preserve their authenticity while adapting to modern dining expectations. What started as a small family restaurant has grown into a beloved culinary destination, but our commitment to traditional flavors remains unchanged.

"Good food is not just about taste; it's about memory, tradition, and the love shared around the table."

- Fatima Al Hassan, Family Matriarch

Meet Our Founders

Brothers Abbas and Mohammad combine decades of culinary expertise with a passion for hospitality. Abbas, trained in traditional Yemani cooking by family background and later on professionally, oversees the kitchen with meticulous attention to detail.

Mohammad, with his background in hospitality management, ensures every guest experiences the warmth of Yemani hospitality. Together, they've created a space where tradition meets innovation, and every meal tells a story.
